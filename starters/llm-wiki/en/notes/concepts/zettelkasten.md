# Zettelkasten

A Zettelkasten (German for slip-box) is a note system: each card holds one idea (see [[atomic-note]]), cards have
fixed numbers, and cards refer to each other.

- The sociologist [[niklas-luhmann]] built one of about 90,000 cards.
- Cards are not filed by subject; numbers and links connect them into lines of thought [[one-idea-per-card^claim-3]].
- Clio's `[[ ]]` links, backlinks and graph can be read as an electronic version of the slip-box.
