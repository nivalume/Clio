# Atomic note

An atomic note contains exactly one idea.

## Key points

- **One idea.** Notes kept by source tie many ideas together and are hard to reuse [[one-idea-per-card^claim-1]]. Split into single ideas, each note can be cited directly [[one-idea-per-card^claim-2]].
- **Your own words.** Restating an idea checks that you understood it.
- **The title is the conclusion.** Write the title as a statement, such as "Links organise notes better than folders", not "Organising notes" [[one-idea-per-card^claim-4]].
- **Organised by links.** Atomic notes form lines of thought through links, and one idea can take part in several of them [[one-idea-per-card^claim-3]].

The [[zettelkasten]] is the best-known practice of this. For the reasoning, see [[why-one-idea-per-note]].
