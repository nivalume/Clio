# Why one idea per note?

An answer filed back after a question: good answers go back into the knowledge base instead of staying in a chat.

## Answer

The smallest thing a link can point to is a note. When a note holds several ideas, other notes can only cite all of
it, and the reader has to find the relevant sentence; the ideas usually belong to different subjects, so no single
place for the note is quite right [[one-idea-per-card^claim-1]].

With one idea per note, a link is precise and the note can appear in several lines of thought
[[one-idea-per-card^claim-3]]. A title that states the conclusion tells the reader what is cited from the link text
alone [[one-idea-per-card^claim-4]].
