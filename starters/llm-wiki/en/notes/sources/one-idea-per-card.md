# One idea per card (example source)

A short essay written for this starter; the original is `raw/one-idea-per-card.md`. This source note records only
what the essay says.

## Claims

- Reading notes kept by source are rarely used again: an idea is tied to everything else from the same source, hard to find and hard to use on its own. ^claim-1
- One idea per card, in your own words, makes a card citable on its own and lets it combine with cards from other sources into new arguments. ^claim-2
- Links between cards matter more than categories: an idea often belongs to several subjects, and a folder can put it in only one place. ^claim-3
- A card's title should stand on its own, ideally as a complete statement, which means writing the conclusion first. ^claim-4
